Do I need to play chords?

You can play melody keys on their own. Add bass and chords when you feel comfortable.

How does practice work?

Note-finding waits for the correct key. Rhythm practice progresses with time.

How do tilt dynamics work?

Start in a comfortable grip. Tilt the top of the phone forward for a softer sound and raise it for a stronger sound. Hold the angle to sustain the level. Recalibrate in bellows settings.

No sound?

Check media volume and Bluetooth output, then try automatic bellows. Reopen the app if needed and contact support if the issue continues.

Where are recordings stored?

Recordings are stored locally in the app. Replay, share or delete them from My Recordings. Export recordings you want to keep before deleting the app.

常见问题

上面的伴奏键必须与旋律一起按吗？

可以只弹旋律。熟悉后，再加入低音和和弦伴奏。

找音练习和节奏跟弹有什么区别？

找音练习会等你按对再继续。节奏跟弹按时间推进，需要跟着提示控制节奏。

体感力度怎么用？

开启时保持舒服的握姿。按住琴键，手机上端前倾渐弱、抬回渐强。保持角度可稳定力度。风箱设置中可以重新校准；信号不可用时会切回自动风箱。

没有声音怎么办？

先检查媒体音量和蓝牙音频输出，切换到自动风箱再试。如果仍无声，请重新打开 App 并联系支持。

录音保存在哪里？

录音保存在本机 App 存储中，可在“我的录音”回听、分享或删除。删除 App 前请先导出需要保留的录音。
