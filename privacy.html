Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026. Applies to Pocket Accordion.

App data

The current app requires no account and includes no advertising, third-party behavioral analytics or developer-operated upload service. Performance recordings are stored locally. Recording captures the app’s instrument output, not ambient microphone audio. You can delete recordings or export them through the system share sheet. Services you select process exported data under their own policies.

Motion data

Tilt dynamics and folding bellows on supported devices process motion or hinge information on your device to control expression. The app does not upload this information to the developer.

Support emails

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond to and resolve your request, through our email provider. Avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information. Contact us to request deletion of personal information in support correspondence, subject to applicable legal or dispute-related retention requirements.

Website and platform services

This website adds no analytics scripts or tracking cookies. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process access logs such as IP addresses. Apple’s store, backup and diagnostic services may process information under your settings and their policies.

Children and changes

We do not actively collect children’s personal information through the app. We will update this policy and its effective date when our practices change.

隐私政策

生效日期 2026 年 11 月 23 日
适用产品 口袋手风琴 / Pocket Accordion

应用中的数据处理

当前版本无需注册账号。我们未在 App 中集成广告、第三方行为分析或开发者运营的数据上传服务。

演奏录音保存在本机 App 存储中。录音功能记录 App 的乐器输出，不录制环境麦克风声音。你可以在 App 内删除录音，或通过系统分享发送到自己选择的服务。接收服务按其自身隐私政策处理数据。

动作数据

体感力度和支持设备上的折叠风箱使用设备动作或合页信息控制演奏力度。这些数据在设备上处理，不由 App 上传给开发者。

联系支持

如你发送支持邮件，我们会收到你的邮箱地址、邮件内容以及主动提供的附件，仅用于回复和处理问题。邮件通过邮箱服务提供商处理。请勿发送不必要的敏感信息。如需请求删除支持邮件中的个人信息，请联系下方邮箱；适用法律或争议处理要求保留的记录除外。

网站和系统服务

本网站未主动设置分析脚本或追踪 Cookie。网站由 GitHub Pages 托管，托管服务可能处理 IP 地址等访问日志。Apple 的商店、系统备份及诊断服务可能按用户设置和其政策处理数据。我们不承诺控制这些服务的独立数据处理。

儿童与政策更新

我们不会通过 App 主动收集儿童个人信息。功能或数据处理方式变更时，会更新此政策和生效日期。
